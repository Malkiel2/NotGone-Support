SHORTCUTS

Make the connection.

One quick setup in Apple Shortcuts, then your archive can begin.

Apple Shortcuts ↗

  1. Choose capture apps

    In Notgone, choose the apps you want to capture and tap Prepare my shortcut.

  2. Share to Shortcuts

    Tap Share to Shortcuts, choose Shortcuts in the share menu, and add Notgone.

  3. Enable Automation

    In Shortcuts, open Notgone, tap Edit, expand each selected app and turn on Automation.

  4. Try a real notification

    Return to Notgone and receive a real notification from a configured app. Your saved copy appears in Inbox.

↳

Help & limitations

Only notifications delivered and passed by your automation can be saved. Notgone cannot retrieve chat history, attachments, or messages it never captured.

Notgone cannot recover notifications that were never captured, or detect when a message is edited or removed in another app.
