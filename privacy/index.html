Privacy policy

AppTunesLabs · Notgone · 2026-10-28

Your archive

Notification text, source identifiers, titles, timestamps, favorites, and tags are stored locally. Notgone does not operate a message-content server or require an account. The archive uses iOS Data Protection and is excluded from device cloud backups.

Your choices

You configure the Shortcuts automation and selected apps. Free stores up to 5 notifications, each for 48 hours. Expired Free copies are removed on the next app or automation activity. Pro protects current unexpired and future captures until you delete them. You can pause capture, export, and delete in Settings. Stop the automation separately in Shortcuts.

Optional analytics

If you opt in, Google Firebase Analytics receives generic usage events, app/device information, and an app-instance identifier. We do not send notification content, names, source identifiers, tags, search terms, or their hashes. Advertising consent remains denied. You can turn analytics off and reset the local analytics identifier in Settings.

Purchases

Apple and RevenueCat process purchases and restore access. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app-user identifier, transaction information, product information, and device/app information. No notification content is sent. Purchase processing is separate from optional analytics.

Sharing and security

Exports leave the app only when you choose a destination. Other apps and recipients handle shared copies under their own policies. Face ID or the device passcode protects the interface. Background capture may run after the first unlock following restart. Notgone cannot prevent every screenshot or guarantee capture of every notification.

Contact and updates

Contact AppTunesLabs at apptuneslabs@gmail.com for privacy questions. This policy may be updated when the app changes. Public policy URLs and vendor disclosures must match the released build.
