Device testing on iPadOS 27.2 found that the earlier preview files did not preserve the capture action’s notification-field mappings. Downloads from this page are paused while the serialization is corrected and checked on the device.

The updated NotGone app has been installed on the test iPad. Automatic source identification and policy filtering are implemented; the imported shortcut still needs its field-mapping and real-notification checks before it is ready.

If you already added an earlier NotGone preview shortcut, keep its automation off until the corrected installer is available.

Bulk source selection is still under investigation. No system-wide notification wildcard has been verified.
