Open this page on the device where NotGone is installed. The general shortcut identifies each notification’s actual source app and lets NotGone filter it before saving.

Add general capture shortcut
  1. Open the downloaded file in Apple Shortcuts and choose Add Shortcut. Source identification, Title, Subtitle, Body, and Date are already wired.
  2. The initial trigger selects Telegram. In the imported shortcut’s notification trigger, choose your source app or use + to add apps. Keep NotGoneSourceApp and the capture action’s field mappings unchanged. You can use this same general shortcut for different source apps.
  3. Turn on Automation and review any system permission prompts and Allow Running When Locked where offered. If replacing an older source-specific shortcut, turn that older automation off to avoid duplicate runs.
  4. Receive a notification with a text preview, then check NotGone’s Capture health. Confirm both a real receipt and the correct source. Importing or running a synthetic storage check does not verify delivery.

NotGone applies its policy before saving: capture must be enabled, excluded sources are ignored, Free saves only your selected source, and Pro permits multiple sources. The updated app quietly filters policy rejections rather than showing a shortcut storage error.

All apps at once: no “Select All” or “Any App” option was found in the inspected macOS 27 picker. That does not establish the iPadOS picker’s behavior. This installer does not claim a system-wide wildcard or automatically include newly installed apps.

Older single-source installers

These older installers use a fixed source identity. Do not add other source apps to their triggers. Use the general installer above for automatic identification.
