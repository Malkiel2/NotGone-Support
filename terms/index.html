Using Notgone

Notgone stores copies of notification text passed by an automation you configure. Use it only for content you are authorized to handle. It does not recover uncaptured messages, access chat databases, or guarantee uninterrupted notification capture. Messaging platforms are not affiliated with Notgone.

Free and Pro

Free stores up to 5 notifications from any configured source for 48 hours each. When full, new notifications are not saved until space becomes available. Pro adds unlimited capture capacity, retention until you delete, search, Saved, and tags. Existing Pro history remains readable, exportable, and deletable if access ends. Device storage and platform limits still apply.

One-time purchase

Pro is a non-consumable, one-time purchase. The store shows the localized price before purchase and charges your Apple Account. Restore purchases using the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple and may revoke Pro access. A lifetime unlock covers the app’s available Pro features, not perpetual operation or future services.

Keep your own copies

The archive is local and excluded from cloud device backups. Export important records. Deleting data and enabled retention can be irreversible. You remain responsible for destinations and recipients you choose when sharing.
