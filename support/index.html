What can Notgone save?

Plain text passed from selected notifications by your Shortcuts automation. It cannot read a global notification history or retrieve past chats, photos, or voice messages.

Why is a notification missing?

The source must deliver a notification and your automation must pass its fields. Muted chats, hidden previews, Focus, in-app conversations, disabled automation, or storage problems can prevent capture.

Does it detect edits or removals?

No. Free copies expire after 48 hours. Pro copies stay until you delete them. Notgone does not receive a reliable edit or deletion signal from messaging apps.

Can I move to another iPhone?

The archive is excluded from device cloud backups. Export important records yourself. This version does not import or synchronize archives. Losing this iPhone may mean losing the archive.

How do I stop capture?

Pause Capture enabled in Settings, and disable or delete the corresponding automation in Shortcuts. Deleting Notgone data does not remove your shortcut.
